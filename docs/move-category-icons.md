# Move category icons

The UI now uses antialiased vector paths in `move_category_badge.dart`, drawn
to resemble the physical burst, special concentric ovals and split status oval.
They are interpretations, not original official vector assets. The PNGs below
were used as visual references and are no longer displayed in the category UI.

The bundled category images are the existing Pokémon category sprites distributed
by Pokémon Showdown, downloaded on 2026-10-29. They have not been redrawn.

- Physical: https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/categories/Physical.png
- Special: https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/categories/Special.png
- Status: https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/categories/Status.png

Pokémon imagery belongs to its respective rights holders. Pokémon Showdown is
the distribution source, not the owner of the Pokémon artwork.
